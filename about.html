About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: a neighborhood deserves a welcoming place to enjoy handmade bread and share good food. Our sample bakery story celebrates early mornings, patient baking, and familiar faces.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We value fresh ingredients and thoughtful purchasing. Whenever practical, we seek seasonal ingredients and relationships with nearby suppliers. Ask our team about the ingredients in a specific item.
